Language models learn distributions over tokens; useful applications also depend on sound evaluation, efficient training and inference, and reliable access to evidence. These questions cover those foundations through fine-tuning, agents, and retrieval-augmented generation.

<!-- more -->

Study the questions by topic in **Read** mode, or switch to **Practice** to test your recall one question at a time. Each question includes a brief answer and a detailed explanation. Design and behavioral questions offer suggested approaches and response frameworks.

Progress is saved only in your browser. Detailed answers include mechanisms, worked examples, assumptions, and tradeoffs; named technologies refer to the versions discussed in each explanation.
