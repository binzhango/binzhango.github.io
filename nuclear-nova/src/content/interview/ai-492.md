## Question

The agent answers from a customer's knowledge base, which contains outdated and contradictory articles. How do you prevent confidently wrong answers?

## Brief answer

Version knowledge, model authority and validity dates explicitly, and retrieve contradictory evidence instead of silently selecting whichever passage scores highest. The assistant should qualify uncertainty or escalate when sources cannot be reconciled. Better generation cannot repair a knowledge base with no reliable ownership or update process.

## Detailed answer

**Suggested approach.** Ingestion stores source owner, publication and effective dates, supersession links, and product/region scope. Retrieval favors applicable current authoritative sources while retaining conflict indicators. For a policy question, require the answer to identify the applicable version and cite its exact passage; old documents remain searchable as history, not default guidance.

Route unresolved conflicts to a content owner and record the resolution as a versioned update. Do not let the model invent a hierarchy between two equally authoritative articles. Measure stale-answer rate, contradiction detection, abstention precision, and time to propagate corrections. Test future-dated policy changes, partial rollouts, conflicting regional rules, and an obsolete article with stronger lexical matches. A feedback button should capture the cited version so a later correction can be audited.

### Resolve applicability before recency

Suppose an old policy says a customer can return an item within thirty days, while a newer document says fourteen days. The newer document may apply only to a different region or product tier. Ranking solely by publication date can therefore select the wrong rule. Retrieve the scope and effective date along with the statement, then determine which version applies to the user's situation.

If two applicable authoritative sources disagree, preserve both and mark the decision unresolved. The model can summarize the conflict and ask for the missing constraint, but should not choose whichever text sounds more confident. A content-owner workflow should link the correction to the superseded record and invalidate derived caches.

Evaluate with paired cases that differ only in region, purchase date, or policy version. The answer should change when the governing condition changes and remain stable when irrelevant wording changes. Track false confidence separately from abstention: a system that escalates every question avoids some errors but still fails to provide a useful service. Good knowledge governance makes that tradeoff less severe.
