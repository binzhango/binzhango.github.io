## Question

Give me an example of a project where you used data and machine learning. What obstacles did you hit?

## Brief answer

Choose a real project and explain the problem, data, baseline, your decisions, the obstacles and the measured outcome. Make the contribution and limitations explicit; a complex model is not a substitute for a useful result.

## Detailed answer

A response framework is: define the decision the system supported, explain how labels/features were obtained, identify leakage or quality risks, then describe one difficult obstacle and the evidence used to resolve it. Compare against a simple baseline and report the actual evaluation protocol, not just an impressive metric. Distinguish personal work from teammates' contributions. If an experiment failed, explain what was learned and what changed. Use only genuine experience and verified numbers; an honest smaller project with clear reasoning is stronger than an invented production-scale story.

### Choose an obstacle that changes the technical story

**Illustrative structure:** a team predicts delayed deliveries, but an apparently strong feature is recorded only after the delivery outcome. Removing it lowers the offline score and exposes leakage. The engineer rebuilds a point-in-time dataset, compares a simple baseline, and measures whether predictions arrive early enough to support intervention. This is an example of an obstacle and response, not a project to claim as your own.

In your real story, state the user decision, dataset size and origin at a shareable level, label definition, baseline, and your specific contribution. Explain one failed assumption and the experiment or inspection that revealed it. Give the actual evaluation split and what the result means operationally. If the model never shipped, say so and describe the validated learning rather than implying business impact. Be prepared to discuss alternative solutions and remaining limitations. A credible account shows that you can connect data quality, model behavior, and a useful decision.
