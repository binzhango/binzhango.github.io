## Question

How do you evaluate ASR quality beyond WER, and TTS quality when there is no single correct output?

## Brief answer

WER measures transcription edits but misses whether errors change meaning, named entities or critical numbers. TTS evaluation needs intelligibility, naturalness, pronunciation, speaker consistency and latency because many waveforms can be valid.

## Detailed answer

For ASR, report entity and number accuracy, semantic task success, diarization and performance by accent/noise slice. For TTS, combine blinded listener ratings with targeted pronunciation checks and downstream comprehension. Evaluate streaming artifacts, clipping and long-form consistency. Reference-based signal metrics alone can penalize a natural alternative voice, while average opinion scores can hide rare but serious pronunciation failures.

WER is $(S+D+I)/N$, where substitutions, deletions and insertions are divided by the number of reference words. It treats a wrong account number and a harmless filler-word error similarly, despite different consequences. Add named-entity accuracy, number/date accuracy, intent preservation and downstream task success. Report results by accent, noise, language and recording device, and inspect diarization errors when speaker identity matters.

For TTS, there is no uniquely correct waveform for a sentence. Use blinded listening tests for intelligibility and naturalness, paired preference comparisons, pronunciation tests for domain terms and comprehension questions for critical content. Measure speaker consistency across long responses and changes in emotion or emphasis. Automated signal or embedding metrics can assist triage but do not replace listening for important cases.

Streaming needs its own evaluation: time to first audio, sentence-boundary artifacts, clipping, pauses, buffer underruns and interruption stop time. A system with a strong average naturalness score can still mispronounce a rare medication or omit a negation. Weight those errors according to the application and include them deliberately in the test set. Keep recording conditions and listener instructions controlled so changes reflect the system rather than different evaluation setups.
