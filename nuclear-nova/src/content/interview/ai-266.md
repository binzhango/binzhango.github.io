## Question

What is multi-token prediction (MTP) and why train with it?

## Brief answer

Multi-token prediction adds training targets for several future tokens at each position instead of supervising only the immediate next token. It can improve representation learning and provide prediction modules useful for speculative generation, at additional training cost.

## Detailed answer

A shared backbone may feed distinct future-token heads, while other designs connect prediction modules sequentially; do not assume all MTP architectures are identical. Mask targets near sequence/document boundaries so the model is not rewarded for predicting unrelated examples. Compare at matched training compute as well as token count to separate richer supervision from more arithmetic. At inference, extra heads may be discarded or used as drafts that the main next-token model verifies; naive parallel emission does not preserve the original autoregressive distribution.

### More future targets change the learning signal

Ordinary next-token training predicts $x_{t+1}$ from the prefix through t. An MTP objective also adds weighted losses for later targets, for example $x_{t+2}$ and $x_{t+3}$, using the particular architecture's conditioning rules. This encourages the shared representation to support a longer continuation, but also adds parameters or computation and can introduce competing objectives if weights are poorly chosen.

For a packed training batch, mask targets that cross document boundaries; otherwise a future-token head learns to predict unrelated text from the next example. Compare quality at both equal tokens and equal compute, because an apparent gain might simply reflect extra training work. At serving time, auxiliary predictions can form a draft that the main target model verifies in parallel. Acceptance, verification cost, and draft overhead determine whether this reduces latency. The heads can also be omitted from ordinary next-token serving. Multiple predicted tokens are not automatically valid samples from the original autoregressive model, so correctness requires the appropriate verification algorithm.
