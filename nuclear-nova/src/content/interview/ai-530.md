## Question

Design discovery and search across millions of user-created characters.

## Brief answer

Combine text search, semantic retrieval, structured character attributes, and policy-aware ranking. Separate discovery of an appealing character from faithful long-term conversation behavior. User-generated names and descriptions are untrusted content, and popularity alone can amplify spam or unsafe material.

## Detailed answer

**Suggested approach.** Index names, descriptions, language, creator-provided tags, moderation state, and engagement features with versioned updates. Use lexical search for exact names and semantic retrieval for intents such as “patient history tutor.” Apply eligibility and age-appropriate policy before ranking, then diversify results across relevant styles and creators.

Cold-start characters need content-based ranking and bounded exploration. Detect duplicate spam and manipulated engagement without requiring established popularity to receive any exposure. Evaluate relevance judgments, successful conversation starts, return satisfaction, diversity, and safety violations. Keep private conversations out of public character indexes. Test adversarial descriptions, near-identical names, language mismatch, and policy changes that must remove a character from cached recommendations promptly.

### Build two ranking stages with clear constraints

Candidate generation retrieves exact names, language matches, and semantically relevant character descriptions. Eligibility filtering removes unpublished, restricted, or policy-incompatible entries before the ranking stage. The final ranker balances relevance, quality, freshness, and diversity, with exploration opportunities for new characters that lack interaction history.

For a search such as “patient Spanish tutor,” an exact celebrity-name match is less useful than a character configured for language practice at the right level. Evaluate whether the resulting conversation actually performs that role, not just whether the description repeats the query. Creator-written descriptions can be misleading or adversarial, so claims about capabilities may need validation or user feedback.

Deduplicate spam variants while retaining legitimate differences in style and audience. Monitor creator concentration and repeated exposure because pure engagement optimization can hide useful new entries. Keep private chat content out of public retrieval features unless a separate, explicit policy supports that use. When a character becomes ineligible, invalidate cached recommendations and direct-entry routes as well as search results; removing only the indexed document leaves other discovery paths open.
