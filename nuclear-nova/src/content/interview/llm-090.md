## Question

How to make the model learn more knowledge during pretraining?

## Brief answer

Improve knowledge learning through reliable coverage, diverse contexts, appropriate model capacity, and a training budget that actually lets the model absorb the data.

## Detailed answer

Deduplicate excessive repetition without removing every useful recurrence, balance rare topics, and keep tokenization and optimization healthy. Repeated facts in varied contexts can support learning, but adding more text does not guarantee reliable factual recall. Evaluate held-out questions and paraphrases, not just corpus likelihood. For facts that change frequently or require exact attribution, retrieval may be more maintainable than storing everything in parameters.

A fact is easier to learn usefully when it appears in explanatory context and connects to related concepts. Merely repeating the same sentence may encourage exact-string memorization. For example, varied descriptions of a protocol should connect its purpose, message sequence, failure conditions, and implementation consequences rather than repeat a one-line definition thousands of times.

The training system must also have enough capacity and effective updates to absorb the material. If domain passages are a tiny fraction of the token mixture, important concepts may receive little exposure. If tokenization fragments critical notation excessively, sequences become longer and the model has a harder prediction problem. Balance sources and inspect whether losses and downstream performance improve on the intended material.

Evaluate recall under paraphrases, changes in question framing, and combinations of facts. Test whether the model distinguishes similar entities and versions rather than supplying a memorized nearby answer. Knowledge represented in parameters is difficult to inspect and update precisely, so use retrieval when exact provenance or frequent changes matter. Better next-token loss is evidence of improved predictive fit, not sufficient evidence that every supplied fact can be recalled correctly.
