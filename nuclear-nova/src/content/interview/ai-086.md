## Question

How would you audit a deployed model for differential performance across user groups?

## Brief answer

Measure performance by relevant user groups and intersections using appropriate, authorized data and a clearly defined harm model. Report uncertainty and task context; equal aggregate accuracy does not imply equal impact.

## Detailed answer

Compare error types, calibration, refusal rates and downstream outcomes, accounting for sample size and label quality. Inspect whether disparities come from data coverage, measurement bias or different operating conditions. Do not infer sensitive attributes from weak proxies merely to fill a dashboard. Test mitigations on held-out data and monitor for regressions. Some fairness objectives conflict mathematically, so document the chosen objective and decision context.

Define the decision and harm before choosing metrics. For a speech recognizer, word and entity errors by accent may be relevant; for a support assistant, unjustified refusals or incorrect resolution rates may matter more. Use authorized, appropriately collected group information and report uncertainty for small samples rather than filling gaps with unreliable inferred attributes.

Compare both aggregate and intersectional slices, but inspect whether labels and operating conditions differ. A group using noisier microphones may experience an acoustic failure, while another may face missing language coverage or biased ground-truth labels. These causes need different remedies. Include calibration and error severity, since equal accuracy can conceal very different false-positive and false-negative burdens.

Where base rates differ, some fairness criteria cannot all hold simultaneously except under special conditions. State which objective fits the application and why, rather than claiming one number establishes fairness. Test candidate mitigations on held-out data and examine whether improving one slice harms another. Pair quantitative results with expert or affected-user review where appropriate. After deployment, monitor distribution changes and provide a route to correct harmful decisions. The audit should produce a causal investigation and measurable remediation plan, not just a demographic dashboard.
