## Question

Design a recommendation system for Facebook Ads, and an evaluation framework for ads ranking.

## Brief answer

Ads ranking combines relevance and predicted outcomes with auction, budget and policy constraints. Evaluate calibration and advertiser/user outcomes together; increasing click-through rate alone can reward misleading ads or waste advertiser spend.

## Detailed answer

Build eligible candidates from targeting and consent-compatible signals, estimate click/conversion probabilities, then apply the auction and pacing rules explicitly. Delayed conversions require attribution windows and labels that avoid treating every recent non-conversion as a permanent negative. Monitor calibration by advertiser, placement and time, as bids multiplied by miscalibrated probabilities distort allocation. Offline tests include ranking, calibration and leakage checks; online experiments assess incremental advertiser value, user complaints, revenue and latency. Keep policy eligibility separate from predicted profitability. This proposed architecture does not claim access to Facebook's current auction implementation.

### Prediction and allocation interact

An ad request first filters campaigns by consent-compatible eligibility, policy, budget, and placement. The model estimates relevant outcomes; the allocation mechanism combines them with bids and the declared auction rules. Budget pacing controls how quickly a campaign spends across the day. These components must be evaluated together because a calibrated predictor can still be used by a poor pacing policy.

For an illustrative value-based score, a $2 conversion bid multiplied by a predicted 0.05 conversion probability gives $0.10 expected value before other terms. A probability overestimate systematically overvalues that campaign. Calibrate by relevant slices and account for delayed conversions rather than labeling all fresh impressions negative. Use offline log-loss/calibration and ranking checks, then experiments measuring incremental advertiser outcomes, user experience, revenue, and spend stability. Clicks and attributed conversions are not automatically causal lift. Watch feedback loops in exposure and advertiser selection, and preserve independent policy enforcement so a profitable prediction cannot make a prohibited ad eligible.
