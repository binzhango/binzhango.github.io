## Question

What are some major difficulties or limitations commonly faced when building machine learning systems?

## Brief answer

Common difficulties include poor data quality, distribution shift, leakage, inadequate evaluation, underfitting, overfitting, and deployment constraints.

## Detailed answer

A model can fail even with low training loss: labels may be inconsistent, the target may poorly represent the product goal, or production inputs may differ from training inputs. Latency, memory, privacy, and feedback loops also matter. Diagnose errors by relevant slices, inspect the data pipeline, and compare with a simple baseline before changing model capacity.

A practical diagnosis separates data, modeling, and operation. Suppose a support classifier appears excellent offline but routes new requests badly. First inspect whether training labels were assigned consistently and whether evaluation accidentally included replies written after the outcome. Then check representation: truncating each ticket before the error message may remove the predictive information entirely. Finally compare deployment preprocessing with the training pipeline.

Even a correct model can fail operationally. A feature service may return stale values, a rare category may arrive in a new language, or the chosen threshold may overload the human review queue. Collect error examples with input version, prediction, and eventual outcome so these explanations can be distinguished.

A useful response is to rank failures by frequency and consequence, establish a baseline, fix the largest verified cause, and reevaluate the same slices. More parameters are appropriate when capacity is the limitation; they do not repair missing labels, an unsuitable objective, or unavailable production features. Deployment requires a fallback and ongoing measurement because the environment can change after a successful launch.
