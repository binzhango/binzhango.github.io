## Question

Every assertion in a Harvey answer needs to link back to a specific passage. Design the grounding system, and tell me how you would measure the unsupported-claim rate.

## Brief answer

Require each factual assertion to reference immutable source spans, then verify both citation validity and support. Store source version, offsets, and document/page coordinates so the user can inspect the evidence. Treat derived conclusions as explicit inferences with cited premises rather than pretending a source states them verbatim.

## Detailed answer

**Suggested approach.** Generate structured claims with evidence IDs from an allowed retrieved set. Deterministically reject unknown or inaccessible IDs, then check entailment, numbers, negation, and scope. Unsupported claims are revised, removed, or marked unresolved. Keep source extraction quality visible because a correct citation to corrupted OCR is still unreliable evidence.

Define unsupported-claim rate as unsupported assessable assertions divided by all assessable assertions, with separate severity and missing-citation metrics. Use blinded professional review to estimate the verifier's false-negative rate and agreement. Sample both random outputs and high-risk findings; do not report only cases selected by the verifier. Test partially supported compound sentences, misleading quotations, amended agreements, and conclusions spanning several documents. This proposed grounding design does not assert any vendor's private implementation.

### Define the denominator before measuring

Split compound sentences into independently assessable assertions. “The agreement lasts three years and renews automatically” requires support for duration and renewal, not one citation attached to the sentence. Label each assertion supported, partially supported, contradicted, or unverifiable under a documented rubric. Track missing evidence separately from a source that actively contradicts the claim.

If a reviewed sample contains 400 assessable claims and twelve are unsupported, the raw rate is 3%. Report sample selection and uncertainty, and separately count whether those twelve include high-impact conclusions. Oversampling difficult cases is useful for finding failures but requires weighting before estimating a population rate.

The verifier should preserve the exact source span and version used for its decision. If the agreement is later amended, the old answer remains reproducible and can be marked stale rather than silently pointing to different text. For calculated or synthesized conclusions, store the premises and transformation. A functioning evidence link is a navigation property; support and applicability are separate properties that require substantive checking.
