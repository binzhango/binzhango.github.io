## Question

Compare cross-entropy, perplexity, and BLEU. When is perplexity misleading?

## Brief answer

Cross-entropy measures token prediction loss; perplexity exponentiates that loss; BLEU measures clipped n-gram overlap with reference translations.

## Detailed answer

For mean NLL $H$ measured with natural logs, $\mathrm{PPL}=e^H$. BLEU combines modified n-gram precision with a brevity penalty and does not need model probabilities. It can reward high overlap despite a crucial factual error and penalize valid paraphrases.

Compare perplexities only with compatible tokenization, evaluation text, context, and masking. Fluency alone does not determine a sentence's perplexity, and changing “cat” to “dog” does not establish either a perplexity or BLEU score. Autoregressive encoder-decoder models also have well-defined conditional perplexity. Evaluate factuality and task success separately.

Perplexity is the geometric inverse of the probabilities assigned to observed tokens. If a model gives every observed token probability $1/4$, its mean loss is $\log4$ and perplexity is 4. It behaves, in that limited sense, as if choosing among four equally likely alternatives. This does not mean it literally considers only four vocabulary entries at each position.

Tokenization changes the prediction units. A rare word split into four subwords contributes four targets, whereas a tokenizer that encodes it as one token contributes one. Raw per-token perplexities from those systems are not directly comparable. Repeated boilerplate can also lower corpus perplexity while important factual questions remain unanswered.

BLEU evaluates generated text rather than next-token probabilities. Clipping prevents a repeated word from earning unlimited credit, and the brevity penalty discourages excessively short outputs. Nevertheless, a translation that changes “not approved” to “approved” can retain high overlap. For an assistant, combine likelihood or overlap measures with task correctness, evidence support, and appropriately designed human evaluation.
