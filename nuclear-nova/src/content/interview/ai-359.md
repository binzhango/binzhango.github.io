## Question

You're shipping notification summarization to hundreds of millions of users in 30+ locales, and you cannot log user content. Design the evaluation and regression-detection story.

## Brief answer

Suggested approach: Build a strong consented offline evaluation corpus and complement it with privacy-preserving operational signals from devices. Without logging content, aggregate usage can detect some regressions but cannot explain every factual error. Use locale-specific expert review, synthetic edge cases, opt-in feedback, and controlled rollouts, while making the remaining observability limits explicit.

## Detailed answer

Evaluate notification groups for factual accuracy, attribution, negation, urgency, and preservation of sensitive details. Include native examples across locales, scripts, short messages, sarcasm, and multiple senders. Test model, prompt, tokenizer, and OS/runtime changes as a versioned combination.

On-device checks can report bounded aggregate categories such as latency failures, truncation, or schema errors, subject to privacy controls. Track opt-outs and corrections carefully without assuming their absence means correctness. Canary by locale and device class, with rollback criteria for severe failures. Provide an accessible way to inspect originals and report issues voluntarily. Keep a consented reproduction path for rare failures rather than claiming anonymous counters can measure all summary quality.

### Combine offline evidence with private operational signals

Create a consented evaluation corpus with native notification examples and exact required facts. A group containing “delivery delayed” followed by “delay resolved” should summarize the latest state; a message quoting an accusation should not turn the accusation into fact. Annotate sender, chronology, negation, and urgency separately to catch these failures.

On-device aggregate counters can detect crashes, truncation, unsupported languages, and latency regressions without collecting content, but they cannot determine whether a fluent sentence reversed a meaning. Maintain a voluntary issue-reporting path and periodic consented research for that gap. Canary each model/template/runtime bundle across device and locale slices, retaining a known-good rollback. Evaluate severe errors with targeted tests rather than relying on sparse random sampling alone. A locale with few reports may have fewer users or lower reporting propensity, so absence of complaints is weak evidence. Keep original notifications accessible so users can inspect important details.
