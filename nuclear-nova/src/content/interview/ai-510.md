## Question

An agentic research query returns a memo citing a case that was overruled. Where does that get caught?

## Brief answer

Catch negative treatment during retrieval and again before finalizing the memo, using an authoritative legal-history source tied to jurisdiction and date. A semantic search result or language model's memory is insufficient to establish whether a case remains usable authority. Surface unresolved status for professional review instead of silently treating the citation as valid.

## Detailed answer

**Suggested approach.** Normalize case identities, retrieve treatment relationships, and store the status lookup timestamp with the cited passage. Distinguish overruling, reversal, criticism, and limited treatment rather than collapsing everything into a binary flag. Check whether the specific proposition relied on is affected and whether the jurisdictional context matches the task.

A prepublication validator checks every authority in the generated memo, including cases introduced during synthesis. If the authority is affected, revise the argument, find appropriate support, or flag the unresolved issue. Test changed status, ambiguous case names, incomplete coverage, and a service outage. Measure missed negative treatment and unsupported legal propositions with qualified reviewers. This is an engineering control for evidence quality, not a substitute for legal analysis.

### Make authority checking a required stage

The retrieval stage should attach treatment metadata to each normalized authority, while the final validator enumerates every case actually cited in the memo. That second enumeration matters because generation can introduce an authority that was not in the original retrieved bundle. Match citations to stable identities rather than relying only on short names shared by several cases.

Negative treatment must be tied to the proposition and task date. A later decision can reject one holding while leaving another proposition relevant, or the user may be asking a historical question. The system should surface the relationship and affected passage for a qualified reviewer rather than attempting to reduce all legal status to a model-generated green check.

If the treatment service is unavailable or its coverage is incomplete, mark the authority check unresolved and block any claim that the memo has been fully verified. Preserve the lookup time and source version. A regression test should change the treatment relationship while keeping the case text unchanged and verify that the memo's status changes. This tests the control path without substituting software for professional legal judgment.
