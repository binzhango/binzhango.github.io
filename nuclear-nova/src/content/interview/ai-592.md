## Question

Design a system to improve traffic in NYC.

## Brief answer

Define the objective and constraints before optimizing traffic: person throughput, bus reliability, emergency access, pedestrian safety, emissions, and equitable delay can conflict. Start with a bounded corridor and an auditable control strategy rather than promising a citywide AI optimum. Use simulation and supervised pilots before changing live signal behavior.

## Detailed answer

**Suggested approach.** Fuse aggregate counts, signal states, transit arrival data, incidents, and road constraints with privacy safeguards. Estimate queues and demand, then optimize coordinated signal plans within deterministic safety rules for clearance times, pedestrian phases, and emergency priority. Keep a tested fallback plan if sensors or optimization fail.

Evaluate travel-time distributions, bus punctuality, pedestrian wait, spillback, and safety indicators across neighborhoods and times. Compare with the existing timing plan using a staged experiment, accounting for weather and demand changes. Avoid improving one intersection by moving congestion downstream. The deployment requires coordination with the responsible transport authority; legal operating constraints and safety approval are inputs to engineering, not rules for the model to invent.

### Optimize a bounded corridor first

Select several connected intersections with measurable bus delay or queue spillback. Ingest signal states, aggregate vehicle/pedestrian counts, transit arrival estimates, and incidents. A state estimator identifies queues and arrival rates; an optimizer proposes phase durations or offsets within an authority-approved safety envelope. A deterministic controller enforces minimum clearances and fallback plans independently of the optimizer.

Evaluate person throughput rather than vehicle throughput alone so a bus full of passengers is not treated as equivalent to one car. Include pedestrian waits, accessibility, emergency response, and downstream spillback. Simulate demand and sensor failures, then run a supervised pilot with a stable comparison and appropriately matched times. A local improvement can merely move congestion to a neighboring street, so evaluate network effects. Preserve privacy through aggregate sensing and bounded retention. The engineering output is a tested control policy with monitoring and rollback, not an unconstrained model directly changing live signals. Actual operating rules and approvals come from the responsible authority.
