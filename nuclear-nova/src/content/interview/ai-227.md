## Question

Design the ML components behind an Instagram Story feature.

## Brief answer

Clarify the Story feature before choosing models: ordering, creative effects, captions and harmful-content detection are different tasks. For a typical Story tray, combine relationship/freshness signals with predicted viewing value and independent safety checks.

## Detailed answer

Use separate pipelines for content understanding and ranking, sharing versioned features where appropriate. Ephemeral content demands quick ingestion and deletion propagation; stale recommendations can point to expired Stories. Captioning needs speech/language coverage, while visual effects need on-device latency and stability testing. For ordering, evaluate skips, replies and repeated exposure alongside explicit user preference signals. Handle new creators and sparse histories through fallbacks and exploration. Test privacy rules for close-friends or restricted audiences before content is made eligible. Identify one measurable user outcome for each model rather than presenting a collection of unrelated classifiers as a product design.

### Scope a complete Story-tray path

For ordering Stories, ingestion validates access and expiry, extracts content features, and indexes eligible items by creator. The online service retrieves Stories from allowed relationships, scores likely viewing value using recency and relationship signals, and reranks to reduce repeated exposure. Check permissions again at delivery because close-friends lists or sharing settings may have changed after indexing.

Creative effects and captions have different contracts. Effects need stable tracking and low on-device frame latency; captions need synchronized speech recognition and language coverage. Share useful embeddings or media processing infrastructure without combining unrelated quality objectives into one metric. Expired media must disappear from caches and review queues as well as the primary store. For the tray, measure successful viewing, skips, replies, and explicit preferences; for captions, measure timing and word/entity accuracy; for effects, measure visual stability and device performance. Test weak connectivity, missing audio, new creators, and sparse history. The system is complete only when models connect to a reliable upload–delivery–expiry lifecycle.
