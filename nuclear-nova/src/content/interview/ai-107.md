## Question

Design the serving stack for a consumer chat assistant at hundreds of millions of users.

## Brief answer

Separate global request admission, regional routing, model-specific GPU pools and streaming delivery. Plan capacity from concurrent active tokens and latency targets rather than the total registered-user count.

## Detailed answer

Forecast arrival distributions, prompt/output lengths and peak regional demand. Use continuous batching, cache-aware routing, cancellation and strict admission limits; degrade to a smaller evaluated model or queue transparently when saturated. Keep conversation storage independent of inference workers and design reconnect behavior for interrupted streams. Track useful SLO-compliant throughput, cache memory, retry amplification and cost per completed task. Regional failures need spare capacity or deliberate load shedding, not optimistic unlimited failover.

For a hypothetical peak of 10,000 requests/s with 500 output tokens each, demand is five million output tokens/s. If a measured replica sustains 5,000 output tokens/s at the target latency, the arithmetic floor is 1,000 replicas before headroom, failures, and prefill capacity. These are illustrative inputs, not claimed hardware results. Change each assumption independently in load tests; daily users alone cannot determine this fleet size.

Keep the control plane separate from the token-serving data plane. The control plane manages model releases, capacity, quotas and health. Regional ingress authenticates requests and assigns them to a model pool using prompt length, service tier, cache locality and available KV capacity. Workers perform continuous batching and stream tokens through a delivery layer that supports cancellation and explicit reconnect semantics.

A conversation store retains messages and confirmed tool results independently of GPU workers, so a worker restart does not erase the session. Avoid automatically replaying action-taking turns after failure; generation can be retried, but remote effects need an action ledger. Autoscaling should anticipate model-loading time and traffic bursts rather than waiting until every queue is full.

Plan failure capacity explicitly. Losing one region may exceed spare capacity elsewhere, requiring admission limits, a smaller evaluated model or transparent degradation. Load-test mixed prompts and outputs, cache-cold traffic, reconnect storms and mass cancellations. Report goodput within TTFT and inter-token objectives alongside token throughput. The unit economics include idle reserve, cache memory, networking, moderation, tools and retries, so cost per successful conversation is more informative than one accelerator's peak token rate.
