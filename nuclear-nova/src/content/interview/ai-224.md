## Question

Design the recommendation system for Instagram Reels.

## Brief answer

Build a multi-stage short-video recommender: candidate retrieval, ranking and constrained reranking, informed by fresh interaction and content features. Optimize useful long-term engagement with quality and safety guardrails rather than watch time alone.

## Detailed answer

This is a proposed design, not a description of Instagram's internal implementation. Retrieve from followed creators, collaborative signals, content similarity and exploration pools. A ranking model predicts several outcomes, including completion, skipping and explicit negative feedback. Correct for exposure bias and account for video length; raw watch time favors long clips. Rerank for diversity, freshness, creator coverage and policy constraints. Cold-start videos need content representations and controlled exploration. Offline ranking gains must survive a user-level randomized experiment that checks satisfaction, harmful exposure and latency. Log feature/model versions to detect stale features and training-serving skew.

### Separate discovery from expensive ranking

An online request retrieves candidate videos from several sources: followed creators, embedding neighbors, collaborative patterns, and an exploration pool. Apply visibility, availability, and policy eligibility before scoring. A heavier ranker predicts outcomes such as viewing satisfaction, early skip, sharing, and explicit dislike, while a final stage limits repetition and balances freshness and diversity.

The ingestion path computes visual/audio/text representations and updates recent interaction features with versioned timestamps. A newly uploaded video has little engagement history, so content features and controlled exposure establish its audience. Long watch duration can indicate enjoyment or merely a long clip; normalize outcomes and inspect repeated rewatches, accidental pauses, and negative feedback. Use chronological offline splits and an online experiment with user-level or appropriate clustered assignment. Measure candidate recall separately from ranking so missing content is not blamed on the scorer. Keep a stable fallback and monitor feature freshness; a sophisticated ranker with stale recent-interest features can perform worse than a simple current baseline.
