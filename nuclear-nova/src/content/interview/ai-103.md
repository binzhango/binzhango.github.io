## Question

Design a document-intelligence pipeline: scanned PDFs in, structured fields out, at 10M documents.

## Brief answer

Process documents through durable, idempotent stages: classification, text/OCR extraction, layout analysis, field extraction, validation and review. At ten million documents, retries, provenance and exception handling must be designed from the start.

## Detailed answer

Store the original file and immutable processing version, queue page-level work where possible, and checkpoint expensive OCR. Validate types, totals, units and cross-field constraints after extraction; attach source coordinates to every important field. Route uncertain or inconsistent outputs to reviewers rather than fabricating values. Measure field accuracy by document family, cost per page and backlog age. Reprocessing should create a new version without silently changing previously approved records.

Store original documents immutably with content hashes and access metadata. A durable queue drives classification, page rendering/OCR, layout recovery, field extraction and validation. Cache expensive intermediate results by document and parser version so a changed field extractor does not require repeating every OCR step. Use bounded retries and a dead-letter queue for corrupt files or unsupported formats.

For an invoice, extraction should return vendor, date, currency, line items and totals with source coordinates. Deterministic checks can verify that line totals plus tax reconcile with the invoice total and that dates and units have valid formats. A plausible value with no supporting span should be marked missing or uncertain. Reviewers need the highlighted original region, not just a generated confidence score.

At ten million documents, track throughput in pages as well as files because lengths vary widely. Partition work by document family and priority, measure backlog age and budget for reprocessing after parser fixes. Version approved outputs so later runs do not silently overwrite audited records. Evaluate field-level precision/recall and document-level success by template, language and scan quality. A high average OCR score does not establish that critical totals or account identifiers are correct.
