## Question

Walk me through DeepSeekMoE. How is it different from a standard top-2 MoE like Mixtral?

## Brief answer

DeepSeekMoE combines more finely divided routed experts with always-active shared experts. The intent is to let routed experts specialize while shared experts capture broadly useful computation, whereas a standard Mixtral-style layer selects two larger routed experts.

## Detailed answer

The DeepSeekMoE paper studies these two design choices. Fair comparison holds active compute or other resource budgets fixed; simply adding experts and reporting a quality gain does not isolate specialization. Fine granularity creates more possible expert combinations but increases routing, dispatch and kernel scheduling complexity. Shared experts consume compute for every token. For deployment, inspect token-load skew, communication and small expert matrix sizes alongside model quality. Expert identities are learned computation paths, not guaranteed human-interpretable topic labels. Use an ablation that removes sharing or changes granularity to test which feature helps on the target workload.

### Compare at the same active budget

A standard top-two MoE selects two relatively large feed-forward experts. Fine-grained segmentation divides that capacity into more smaller experts and can activate several of them, offering more combinations at a similar active computation budget. Shared experts execute for every token, carrying broadly useful transformations so routed experts can specialize more distinctly.

The router scores routed experts, dispatches token vectors to the selected subset, combines weighted outputs, and adds the shared path. A fair ablation holds active FLOPs or another stated budget fixed; activating more experts without shrinking them changes the resource comparison. Smaller experts can lead to tiny matrix operations and more dispatch overhead, while shared experts contribute an unavoidable baseline cost. Total storage still includes every expert. Inspect expert-load distributions, quality, communication, and actual kernel utilization together. Expert names are identifiers for learned functions, not guaranteed categories such as “math” or “law.” The architecture offers capacity choices whose practical benefit must survive systems constraints and task evaluation.
