## Question

How do you take a model trained at 8K–32K and make it work at 128K or more?

## Brief answer

Extend positional handling, expose the model to useful long-context examples, and validate both long- and short-context behavior. RoPE scaling can make positions numerically usable, but reliable long-range reasoning usually needs suitable continued training and evaluation. Serving support must also accommodate the resulting prefill cost and KV cache.

## Detailed answer

Begin with a checkpoint whose license and attention architecture fit the deployment. Choose a position-extension method such as interpolation or YaRN, then progressively increase sequence length using a mixture of short and long examples. Include tasks that require evidence spread across the document; padding short examples to 128K teaches little long-range reasoning. Keep short examples to detect and limit regression.

Evaluate multiple lengths and evidence positions, not only the advertised maximum. Measure document QA, multi-hop retrieval, synthesis, and instructions located far from the answer. Record accuracy alongside memory, time to first token, and decode latency. Compare against retrieval at equivalent latency and cost. Tune positional parameters to this checkpoint; changing a context-limit field alone is not validation. YaRN describes an efficient RoPE context-extension approach.

### Why positional scaling needs validation

RoPE rotates query/key components by position-dependent angles. Feeding positions far beyond those encountered in training changes the phase relationships on which attention learned to rely. Interpolation maps a larger position range into a familiar range; frequency-aware methods adjust components differently so local detail is not compressed uniformly. That makes extrapolation more plausible, but does not teach the model to combine facts separated by a hundred pages.

An extension experiment might start with a 32K checkpoint, add 64K examples, then 128K examples while retaining a short-context mixture. Include documents whose answer requires joining two distant facts, not merely finding a repeated string. Keep the exact same short-task holdout before and after adaptation. If long-context scores improve but ordinary instruction following declines, change the mixture or learning rate rather than accepting the maximum window as the success metric. Record the largest length meeting both quality and latency targets as the usable context.
