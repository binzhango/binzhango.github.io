## Question

Explain streaming TTS chunking and jitter-buffer sizing.

## Brief answer

Streaming TTS balances early playback against linguistic context and network variability. Chunk text at stable phrase boundaries, and use a jitter buffer large enough to absorb normal arrival variation without making conversation feel delayed.

## Detailed answer

Very small chunks can break pronunciation and prosody; large chunks increase time-to-first-audio. Estimate arrival jitter from observed packet timing and adapt the buffer within bounded limits. Keep sequence numbers, timestamps and cancellation IDs so late chunks cannot play in a new turn. Handle underruns with controlled pauses rather than replaying stale audio. Measure startup latency, discontinuities and interruption response together.

The text chunk boundary affects both latency and speech quality. A synthesizer receiving “I didn't say she stole...” before the rest of the sentence may choose emphasis that becomes wrong when the sentence completes. Waiting for a full paragraph preserves context but feels slow. A practical segmenter starts with a stable clause or sentence and supplies continuity context to later chunks when supported.

Audio packets need sequence numbers, timestamps and a generation ID. The playback buffer absorbs variations in packet arrival, but every added millisecond also increases turn-taking delay. Choose a target from measured jitter percentiles, then adapt within limits; avoid growing the buffer indefinitely during a congested connection. Distinguish network lateness from synthesis that cannot produce audio faster than playback consumes it.

If the buffer underruns, pause predictably rather than replaying old data or switching to an unrelated segment. On interruption, flush queued audio and reject late packets from the canceled generation. Evaluate first-audio delay, audible seams, prosody consistency, underrun frequency and stop latency together. A buffer that eliminates all gaps by waiting several seconds is unsuitable for conversation, while a near-zero buffer may sound broken under normal network variation.
