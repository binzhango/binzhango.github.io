## Question

Design the safety system for open-ended character chat.

## Brief answer

Use layered safety controls at character creation, discovery, conversation, tool access, and incident response. Separate creative fictional interaction from prohibited conduct and high-risk situations. A single output filter cannot manage long-session manipulation, age-dependent experiences, or unsafe behavior embedded in a character's configuration.

## Detailed answer

**Suggested approach.** Validate character definitions, restrict dangerous capabilities, and apply conversation policies using both current text and relevant context. Use age-appropriate product boundaries where applicable, with privacy-conscious assurance and appeals. Detect repeated escalation patterns and provide suitable handoff or support pathways without presenting the character as a qualified professional.

Evaluate multi-turn adversarial scenarios, policy-consistent benign role-play, multilingual cases, and false refusals. Measure severe violations separately from generic toxicity scores. Maintain versioned policies, incident triage, and the ability to disable a character or feature quickly. Keep sensitive conversation access tightly controlled. The system must be transparent about its limitations and must not optimize dependence or discourage users from seeking human support.

### Evaluate the conversation trajectory

A single reply can look acceptable while a long interaction progressively encourages dependency, unsafe requests, or attempts to evade product boundaries. Keep enough relevant state to recognize these trajectories without indiscriminately retaining every sensitive detail. Policy decisions should consider the current request, age-appropriate experience, character configuration, and recent pattern of interaction.

At creation time, reject configurations that instruct the assistant to bypass platform rules. At serving time, constrain tool capabilities independently of personality text. At discovery time, prevent restricted characters from being recommended to ineligible users. These are separate enforcement points; a final text classifier cannot repair an earlier authorization error or inappropriate recommendation.

Build multi-turn evaluations with benign creative play and boundary-pushing variants, then measure both severe failures and false refusals. Review incidents with access controls and a minimal evidence record, and support rapid disabling of a problematic character version or feature. The architecture should preserve useful fiction and conversation while making nonnegotiable product boundaries enforceable outside a character's instructions.
