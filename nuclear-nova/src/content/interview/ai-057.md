## Question

What is DPO and why did it displace PPO-based RLHF at many labs? When is online RL still better?

## Brief answer

DPO trains directly on preferred/rejected response pairs using a reference policy, avoiding an explicit reward-model-plus-PPO loop. It is operationally simpler in many offline preference settings, but has not universally replaced online RL.

## Detailed answer

Its loss increases the preferred response’s relative log-probability against the rejected response, adjusted by the reference policy and a scale parameter. Offline comparisons limit what behaviors the model learns about. Online RL can generate new candidates and optimize verifiable outcomes beyond the original pair distribution, at higher rollout and stability cost. Compare data quality, exploration needs and evaluation performance rather than selecting by popularity.

For prompt $x$, preferred response $y_w$ and rejected response $y_l$, define the reference-adjusted margin as $m=\log[\pi_\theta(y_w\mid x)/\pi_{ref}(y_w\mid x)]-\log[\pi_\theta(y_l\mid x)/\pi_{ref}(y_l\mid x)]$. DPO minimizes $-\log\sigma(\beta m)$. The update rewards a larger preference margin relative to the reference, with $\beta$ controlling the scale of that objective.

This can be implemented with ordinary likelihood evaluations of paired responses, avoiding the operational complexity of a separately trained reward model, repeated online rollouts and a PPO critic. It still needs a reliable pair dataset and a suitable reference model. Preferences can be noisy or reflect style shortcuts, and training repeatedly on fixed rejected answers does not expose every failure the updated policy might generate.

Online RL is attractive when the model can discover new solutions and receive reliable feedback, such as tests for generated code or verified mathematical answers. It can adapt its sample distribution as the policy improves, at substantial rollout and infrastructure cost. Compare the methods using held-out task success, retention of useful capabilities and total training expense. DPO is a practical offline preference tool; claims that it universally displaced online RL confuse a useful simplification with a general dominance result.
