## Question

Explain how you would auto-label a fleet dataset and what quality controls you would put on it.

## Brief answer

Suggested approach: Use stronger offline models, multi-sensor evidence, and temporal consistency to propose labels, then apply confidence gating and targeted human review. Auto-labeling reduces manual effort but can amplify the teacher's blind spots. Keep an independently human-reviewed holdout and track provenance so model-generated labels do not become unquestioned ground truth.

## Detailed answer

Process complete sequences to exploit future frames and tracking information unavailable to an online model. Fuse calibrated sensors where permitted, generate boxes/tracks/attributes, and flag disagreement between models or modalities. Sample low-confidence, rare, and high-consequence cases for expert adjudication; also audit random high-confidence cases to find systematic mistakes.

Version the labeler, inputs, calibration, and review history. Deduplicate related scenes across train and test, and compare label precision/recall by object size, distance, weather, and occlusion. Retrain on filtered labels and measure improvement on the independent set. Never select labels solely by the student model's agreement with them, because shared errors would pass that test.

### Separate label generation from acceptance

An offline labeler can use future frames, multiple passes, and larger models that an onboard system cannot afford. It proposes a track with 3D boxes across a sequence, then consistency checks flag impossible jumps, size changes, or disagreement between sensors. Human review focuses on uncertain or consequential cases, with a random audit of seemingly easy examples to detect shared blind spots.

Store the proposed label, confidence, model/calibration version, and any human correction separately so errors can be traced and reprocessed. Track precision and missing-object recall; a labeler that produces only obvious objects may have excellent precision while teaching the student to ignore difficult ones. Split by scene and collection episode before labeling-derived augmentation to avoid temporal leakage. Validate student improvements on independently labeled data, including rare weather and small objects. Auto-labeling reduces annotation effort when paired with quality control; it does not convert model predictions into ground truth by declaration.
