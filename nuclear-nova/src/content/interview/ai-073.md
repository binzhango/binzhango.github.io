## Question

What is benchmark contamination and how do you guard against it?

## Brief answer

Benchmark contamination occurs when evaluation content or close variants influence training or repeated model selection. It can inflate scores without improving generalization to genuinely unseen tasks.

## Detailed answer

Track dataset provenance, compare exact and near-duplicate text or code across splits, and split by source, time or entity where needed. Protect hidden tests and avoid continually optimizing against a public leaderboard. Create fresh private tasks and evaluate perturbed variants to probe memorization, while recognizing that perturbations can change difficulty. Absence of a detected match does not prove no contamination, especially when training data is unavailable.

Contamination can occur through exact examples, translated or paraphrased variants, worked solutions, repository test files or repeated human tuning against the same public score. The risk is not limited to copying a question verbatim into pretraining. A team can indirectly overfit a benchmark through many release decisions even when the underlying training corpus was initially clean.

Use exact hashes for identical content, normalized text or token shingles for near duplicates, and source-aware splits to keep related documents or code repositories together. Time-based splits help for changing domains, while entity-based splits prevent repeated customers or products from leaking across train and test. Store provenance so suspicious matches can be investigated rather than merely removed without explanation.

Fresh private tasks and hidden tests provide additional evidence, but secrecy alone is not a complete method: task authors can unconsciously reproduce familiar benchmarks. Perturbing names, numbers or phrasing can reveal brittle memorization, though it may also change genuine difficulty. Report whether training data was available for inspection and what matching methods were used. When data is opaque, avoid claiming contamination has been ruled out. Evaluate transfer to new distributions and complete tasks so a strong result requires more than recognizing a familiar prompt.
