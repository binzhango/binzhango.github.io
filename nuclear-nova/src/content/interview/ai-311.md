## Question

Design a real-time voice agent for a citizen helpline in Hindi and three regional languages, targeting sub-250 ms perceived latency over a phone line.

## Brief answer

Suggested approach: define perceived latency from the user's end of speech to audible acknowledgment, separately from a substantive answer. Sub-250 ms across a phone network is an aggressive target, not a universal guarantee. Stream ASR, overlap intent preparation with speech, use fast endpointing, and stream TTS while preserving correctness and allowing interruption.

## Detailed answer

Budget telephony transport, jitter buffer, speech endpoint detection, ASR finalization, retrieval/model work, and TTS first audio. Measure each at p50 and p95 on real regional networks. A short acknowledgment can begin quickly, but must not claim a transaction completed before confirmation. Critical decisions wait for stable recognition.

Use language-aware ASR, pronunciation lexicons for names and schemes, and a dialog state machine for eligibility or account actions. Handle code-switching, noisy 8 kHz audio, repeated numbers, and barge-in by canceling stale generation and speech. Keep human transfer available with a concise context summary. Evaluate task completion, correction frequency, numeric accuracy, and perceived turn-taking with native speakers; average ASR word error alone misses harmful errors.

### Separate reaction time from answer time

Sub-250 ms perceived latency is most plausible for starting a turn-taking signal or continuing already prepared audio, not completing arbitrary ASR, retrieval, generation, and synthesis after the user stops. Endpoint detection alone can consume much of that budget. Define whether the clock starts at the last speech sample, endpoint decision, or final ASR result; otherwise teams optimize incompatible metrics.

A streaming design sends audio frames to ASR while speech continues, forms provisional intent, and prepares read-only retrieval without committing consequential actions. Once the turn is stable, the dialog manager confirms required entities and streams short TTS chunks. A barge-in detector stops audio playback and cancels stale model work. For “my account number is...,” wait for the complete confirmed identifier before any account action. Budget and test the whole phone network path, including jitter and codec conversion. Report perceived responsiveness separately from time to a correct substantive answer.
