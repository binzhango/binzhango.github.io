## Question

How to improve ranking optimization in Graph RAG?

## Brief answer

Improve Graph RAG ranking by combining entity relevance, relationship relevance, path quality, source support, and ordinary text-retrieval signals.

## Detailed answer

Restrict traversal to useful relation types and hop counts, reduce domination by generic high-degree nodes, and account for dates and extraction confidence. Rerank candidate paths and their supporting passages against the question, then diversify within a token budget. Evaluate answer-supporting evidence rather than only graph connectivity. A short path or popular entity is not automatically the most relevant source.

Rank paths for their ability to answer the question, not merely their existence. For a dependency-owner query, a path using `depends_on` followed by `owned_by` is more relevant than a shorter path through a generic “mentioned_with” edge. Include source freshness and confidence in the extraction, but do not let a numerical confidence substitute for supporting text.

High-degree entities can dominate naive traversal: a widely mentioned organization may connect almost every document while adding little answer-specific information. Restrict relation types, bound hops, and penalize unhelpful generic expansions. Retrieve passages supporting candidate paths and use a query-passage or query-path reranker to evaluate actual support.

Select a diverse evidence set within the token budget. Several paths may repeat one underlying source, while a less prominent path supplies the missing exception or alternate version. Evaluate whether every required relationship reaches the final context and whether generated claims are supported. Compare graph signals with a text-only baseline and perform ablations; better graph connectivity metrics do not automatically mean better answers. Ranking is an evidence-selection problem whose success is measured at the information need.
