## Question

Design a content-moderation system combining classifiers and LLMs.

## Brief answer

Combine fast classifiers for common cases with selective LLM review for contextual ambiguity, and provide human escalation and appeals. Model outputs should drive a policy workflow rather than directly imposing every consequence.

## Detailed answer

Define categories and severity first, then route text, images or video through appropriate detectors. Calibrate thresholds by harm and review capacity; preserve evidence and decision versions for appeal. Test multilingual, adversarial and context-dependent cases, and distinguish detection from policy enforcement. Track false positives, missed harms and reviewer disagreement. Use rollout gates and rollback for model or policy changes because distribution and attacker behavior evolve.

Separate detection from enforcement. A classifier estimates whether content belongs to a category; a policy engine decides whether to allow, limit, remove or send it for review given severity and context. An LLM can help interpret ambiguous cases such as quotation, satire or a threat embedded in a conversation, but its explanation is not proof of a correct decision.

Use a cascade: inexpensive detectors handle clear high-volume cases, uncertain or context-dependent cases go to a stronger model, and consequential ambiguities reach trained reviewers. Preserve the relevant context and decision version so appeals can reconstruct why an action occurred. Do not let a model invent additional evidence or impose an account-level penalty from one uncertain label.

Calibrate thresholds against the cost of missed harm, false positives and available review capacity. Evaluate languages, dialects, adversarial obfuscation and multimodal combinations; aggregate accuracy can hide severe gaps. Monitor appeal reversals and reviewer disagreement as signals of policy or model problems. Roll out new models and policies separately when possible, with replay tests and rollback. The objective is appropriate, explainable enforcement under a clear policy, not maximizing the number of items flagged or minimizing the review queue at any cost.
