## Question

Design the serving stack for a consumer chatbot with real-time search over a social-media firehose.

## Brief answer

Separate fresh social-content ingestion from conversation inference, and retrieve versioned evidence through a low-latency search service before grounded generation. Freshness, deletion propagation and adversarial content are central requirements for a live firehose.

## Detailed answer

Ingest events into a durable log, update keyword/vector indexes and track source-to-index lag. Resolve post visibility and account restrictions before context construction; preserve timestamps so old posts are not described as current. Rank for relevance and source quality, deduplicate reposts, and distinguish verified reports from speculation. Treat retrieved posts as untrusted data and restrict tool authority outside the model. Use deadlines and fallback responses when search is delayed instead of fabricating current facts. Measure evidence support, freshness, p99 latency and cache invalidation, then replay breaking-event spikes and deletion storms during load testing.

### Freshness is part of answer correctness

The ingestion service consumes post creation, edit, deletion, and permission events into a durable log. Indexers update lexical/vector search and expose source-to-index lag. At request time, the gateway authenticates, retrieval filters by visibility and time, a reranker selects evidence, and generation distinguishes established facts from conflicting reports. Recheck sensitive permissions before inserting content into the prompt.

A breaking event can flood the index with reposts and speculation. Cluster duplicate claims, preserve original timestamps and sources, and avoid treating frequency as independent confirmation. If search is stale or unavailable, provide a scoped limitation instead of claiming current knowledge. Cache entries need source/version dependencies so deletions and corrections invalidate affected answers. Trace retrieval, reranking, prefill, and streaming separately, and enforce deadlines under traffic spikes. Evaluate whether answers reflect the latest authoritative evidence and whether deleted/private posts remain inaccessible; token throughput alone says little about a chatbot whose central promise is real-time information.
