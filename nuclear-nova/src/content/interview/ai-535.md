## Question

Design age assurance for a platform where the under-18 experience is fundamentally different.

## Brief answer

Design age assurance around the required product distinction, data minimization, uncertainty handling, and appeal. Separate age verification from identity collection wherever possible, and use a trusted age-band result rather than storing raw identity documents unnecessarily. The applicable requirements must be determined for the deployment's jurisdictions and product by responsible policy and legal owners.

## Detailed answer

**Suggested approach.** Define what evidence is accepted, how long the assertion remains valid, and what happens when age is unknown or disputed. Keep assurance credentials separate from conversation content and expose only the minimum age band to feature gating. Enforce the under-18 experience server-side, including discovery, memory, messaging, and account recovery.

Evaluate false acceptance, false rejection, bypasses, accessibility, and demographic performance of any estimation method. Provide a privacy-preserving appeal path and audit vendor retention and security. Test shared devices, changed accounts, stale credentials, and inconsistent signals. Do not claim that a checkbox or face estimate establishes age with certainty. Product controls should remain safe under uncertainty rather than relying on perfect classification.

### Separate evidence, decisions, and enforcement

Use three components: an assurance provider obtains evidence, a policy service translates that evidence into an age band and expiration, and product services enforce the resulting permissions. A signed assertion might contain an account binding, band, issuer, expiry, and assurance level. The conversation model receives only the permissions it needs, not an identity document or facial image.

Define the unknown state explicitly. An expired assertion must not silently become an adult assertion, and an account recovery flow must not bypass the original assurance requirement. For a disputed classification, let a separate review process issue a replacement assertion; invalidate cached permissions when that happens. Evaluate household accounts and shared devices because knowing the account holder's age does not prove who is currently interacting.

Compare methods by the errors they introduce and the information they collect. Stronger identity evidence can reduce some bypasses while increasing privacy exposure and excluding users without accepted documents. Estimation can be less intrusive but uncertain around the threshold. Select a proportionate combination through the product's reviewed requirements, measure outcomes near the boundary, and preserve a usable appeal route. The central engineering invariant is that every protected action checks current server-side permissions, regardless of what the interface displays.
